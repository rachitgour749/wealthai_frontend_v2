Strategy Logic

Signals are generated at the US market close and aimed at capturing medium-term price swings.

Entry Eligibility

Positions are initiated when an ETF shows confirmed momentum by closing above its technical average.

Daily Close (T) > SMA (T)
Preference: ETFs with the smallest positive distance from SMA.

Exit Hierarchy

  • Profit-Take: Price reverts below SMA after meeting your target threshold.
  • Hard Stop Loss: An automatic exit signal if the asset drops beyond your risk tolerance.
  • Dynamic Capital: Profits are automatically compounds into the next available "swing" slot.
US Market Execution

All trades are precisely synchronized with US Exchange hours:

  • Evaluation: Performance is checked at the NYSE/NASDAQ close (Night in India).
  • Deployment: Validated trades are executed at the subsequent US Market Open.
Regulatory & Compliance

Investments follow the RBI Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) framework:

  • Account funding and asset holdings are managed in USD.
  • Standard LRS limits for individual remittances apply.
  • Automatic reporting for Tax Collected at Source (TCS) where applicable.
