ETF Swing Strategy

A rule-based, medium-term trend following strategy that captures price momentum using technical filters and slot-based portfolio construction.

Executive Summary

The ETF Swing Strategy aims to minimize market volatility impacts while participating in established trends. By focusing on ETFs relative to their Moving Averages, it ensures capital is only deployed when momentum is confirmed.

Trend Filtered

Uses SMA-based technical qualification to separate trends from noise.

Rule-Based Execution

Entries and exits are calculated on EOD data and executed at next-day Open.

Capital Rotation

Dynamic compounding ensures winners contribute more to portfolio growth.

Risk Guarded

Automatic Stop Loss protection active for every single deployment.

Signal Logic

Signals are generated based on the relationship between the daily closing price and the Simple Moving Average (SMA).

Entry Qualification

An ETF is eligible for entry if its daily close is above the trend average. Candidates are then ranked by proximity to the SMA.

Daily Close (T) > SMA (T)
Ranking = (Close - SMA) / SMA (Smallest Positive Preferred)

Exit Conditions

  • Profit Exit: Price crosses below SMA AND profit threshold is reached.
  • Stop Loss: Price drops by more than the configured risk percentage from entry.
  • Retention: If price falls below SMA but profit target isn't met, position is retained until SL is hit.
Portfolio & Capital Rules
  • Slot Allocation: Portfolio is divided into logical units called "slots".
  • Cash Handling: Unused slots remain in cash/liquid assets when trends are absent.
  • Full Compounding: Realized capital is reapportioned across slots for next entries.
Execution Timeline

To reduce slippage and intraday noise, all trade execution follows a fixed T+1 cycle:

  • Day T (Close): Signal generated based on the day's closing price.
  • Day T+1 (Open): Trade executed at the market open price.
