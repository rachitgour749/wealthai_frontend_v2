RS ETF Rotation Strategy

A momentum-driven approach that identifies and holds the strongest performers from the ETF universe by measuring their Relative Strength (RS) against a benchmark.

Executive Summary

The Relative Strength strategy is rooted in the "persistence of performance" principle. It systematically buys ETFs that are outperforming the broader market and sells those that lose momentum, ensuring your portfolio is always aligned with market leadership.

Momentum Focused

Targets high-velocity movements in the market.

Relative Performance

Measures strength against benchmarks, not just absolute gains.

The RS Calculation

We calculate RS across multiple timeframes (1W, 1M, 3M) to identify the "Alpha" generation:

RS = (ETF Return / Index Return) - 1

A positive RS indicates outperformance, while a negative RS signals it's time to rotate into stronger candidates.

Selection & Rebalancing

Our rebalancing engine runs weekly on Friday signals:

  • Ranking ETFs based on composite Alpha scores.
  • Buying the top 'N' performers.
  • Exiting any position that falls out of the leadership rank.
