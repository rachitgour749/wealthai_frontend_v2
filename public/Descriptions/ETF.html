Strategy Insight

ETF Rotation Strategy

A systematic mean reversion approach designed to rotate capital between ETFs, buying at relative lows and capturing price spikes as they revert to historical means.

Executive Summary

The ETF Rotation Strategy leverages statistical mean reversion—the tendency for asset prices to return to their average levels. It identifies ETFs trading near their 52-week lows and rotates capital away from overextended positions, ensuring consistent exposure to value.

Dual-Phase Operation

Phase 1

Accumulation (Weeks 1-52)

Build a robust foundation by investing fixed amounts weekly. We target ETFs closest to their 52-week lows to maximize the average entry price advantage.

Phase 2

Churning (Week 53+)

Transition to active rotation. We sell positions approaching their 52-week highs and redeploy profits into emerging value opportunities at the bottom of the range.

Range Analysis & Selection

We use a 252-day window to calculate price positioning:

  • Distance from Low: Measures undervaluation potential.
  • Distance from High: Measures exit timing for profits.
  • Execution: Automatic orders at Monday market opens.
