ETF Payout Strategy

An income-focused systematic approach designed to generate regular cash flow through Systematic Withdrawals, while maintaining long-term capital preservation.

Executive Summary

Built for investors seeking consistent income, the ETF Payout Strategy bridges the gap between growth and liquidity. It prioritizes capital stability over aggressive gains to ensure a sustainable withdrawal rate over decades.

Predictable Flow

Designed to simulate a "monthly salary" through SWPs.

Volatility Guard

Uses stable, hybrid, or high-yield ETFs to minimize corpus fluctuation.

The Lifecycle

Phase 1: Accumulation

Systematic building of the corpus through SIPs in a diversified ETF basket. Dividends are reinvested to leverage compounding.

Phase 2: Distribution

Transition to Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) mode. Capital is liquidated in small chunks to provide liquid cash while the remaining base continues to grow.

Sustainability Rules

To prevent premature corpus depletion, we recommend:

  • Maintaining a withdrawal rate typically between 3-4% annually.
  • Keeping 12-24 months of payouts in liquid/cash buffer ETFs.
  • Annual rebalancing to reset the target asset allocation.
Disclaimer: Withdrawal rates are subject to market returns. Excessive withdrawal during market downturns can deplete the corpus rapidly. Consult a financial planner.
