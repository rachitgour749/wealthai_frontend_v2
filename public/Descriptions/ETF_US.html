International ETF Strategy

Go global with your investments. This strategy focuses on diversifying your portfolio across international markets through US-listed Exchange Traded Funds, capturing global growth while hedging domestic risks.

Executive Summary

The International ETF strategy provides exposure to the world's largest economy and leading global corporations. It implements the same rigorous mean-reversion or momentum filters used in domestic strategies, adapted for the unique liquidity and hours of international exchanges.

Global Diversification

Access markets beyond India to reduce country-specific risk.

Currency Hedge

Benefit from potential currency appreciation alongside asset growth.

Why US ETFs?

The US stock market represents over 40% of the world's equity market cap. By investing in US ETFs like SPY (S&P 500), QQQ (Nasdaq), or sector-specific funds, you gain direct participation in global tech and healthcare leaders.
  • Unmatched Liquidity: Tight bid-ask spreads even for large volumes.
  • Innovation Exposure: Direct access to the world's most innovative tech giants.
  • Fractional Access: Systematic entry into premium asset classes.
Regulatory Framework (LRS)

Investments in international markets are governed by the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS):

  • Current limit is $250,000 per financial year per individual.
  • TCS (Tax Collected at Source) rules apply as per the latest Finance Act.
  • Reporting requirements for foreign assets in your annual Returns.
Note: International investing involves currency exchange risk and geopolitical considerations. Tax treatment of foreign dividends and gains differs from domestic securities. Consult your tax advisor.
